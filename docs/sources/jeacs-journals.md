# JEACS author calls — Phase 06 source decision

Reviewed 2026-10-29. This is a nonpersonal journal-call semantics pilot in Sinophone humanities/social sciences, not an inferred researcher interest or journal-quality endorsement.

## Current evaluation and permission

Springer Link's [terms](https://link.springer.com/termsandconditions), read directly (266,004 bytes), prohibit systematic automated extraction/indexing including metadata. Its robots permits some collection/journal paths but does not override terms; Nature also redirected to an identity service. No Springer adapter approved. Elsevier's [terms](https://www.elsevier.com/legal/elsevier-website-terms-and-conditions) restrict systematic retrieval/automated downloading without permission. ScienceDirect robots disallows generic crawlers and the public calls path returned an SSO redirect; research-browser access returned 403. No private API, authentication or challenge workaround. MDPI's robots returned 403 directly, while research-browser robots/terms attempts returned 429; rights/access remain unresolved, so no adapter enabled. Its advertised special-issue RSS/explicit submission statuses are promising but insufficient to pass the current gate.

JEACS, the Journal of the European Association for Chinese Studies, is published by EACS and hosted by the University of Vienna's journal platform. The [official about/policy page](https://journals.univie.ac.at/index.php/jeacs/about) supplies journal scope, ISSN 2709-9946, license and fees. The [official announcements](https://journals.univie.ac.at/index.php/jeacs/announcement) expressly encourage distributing JEACS content under CC BY 4.0, and their footer repeats that license. [Robots](https://journals.univie.ac.at/robots.txt) disallows cache/registration for generic agents, not these paths. No separate restrictive automated-read condition was found on the inspected journal pages. Approved: bounded public factual extraction and attributed, clearly rewritten fixtures; no article/website mirror, login, forms or manuscript submission. Preserve journal/EACS attribution, official links, [license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and notice of fixture changes. This is scoped permission evidence, not a blanket platform license or legal certification.

JPC's official announcements also advertise RSS and a standalone manuscript call, but its conference issues have restricted submissions and its article license is different. It is not needed after JEACS proves both category paths. An Open Research Software announcement request timed out; no approved path was established. No second live source is planned absent a demonstrated gap.

## Selected interface and bounds

`jeacs-journal-calls`: one exact announcements GET plus one fixed about/policy GET. At most one fixed reviewed detail GET (announcement 230) when that call appears, to validate directory/detail agreement and preserve stale-entry/conflict behavior. No arbitrary source URL navigation, dynamic endpoint, hidden API, browser or full publisher crawl. All public pages are server-readable HTML; no JavaScript execution is needed. Actual inspected listing 49,674 bytes / 17 announcement cards, policy 16,778 bytes, detail 15,234 bytes: 81,686 decoded UTF-8 bytes across three GETs. Original 174 was separately inspected manually (10,677 bytes), not added to the automatic detail allowlist.

Proposed cadence daily only; no SLA or numerical publisher quota verified. One bounded page/task/run, 15 seconds shared across at most three sequential requests; maximum 32 cards, 128 KiB directory, 64 KiB policy, 128 KiB fixed detail, 512 KiB framed page. Decode through existing bounded UTF-8 HTTP client, deny redirects, bound HTML token/node/depth work before extracting fields. Pin a pure HTML parser; no new ingestion framework. No Cron, delivery or production enablement.

## Classification and identity policy

Accept explicit author manuscript calls only. A thematic special issue/collection is one `journal_special_issue`, including its author CFP. An explicitly unthemed regular-issue call is `journal_cfp`; it does not duplicate a special issue. Published-volume notices, dissertation-list solicitations, generic journal pages and guest-editor/special-issue proposal invitations are not manuscript calls. Unknown/changed structures fail or quarantine; no heuristic journal reputation classification.

Actual standalone examples: [original unthemed 6.2 call 174](https://journals.univie.ac.at/index.php/jeacs/announcement/view/174) and [reminder 190](https://journals.univie.ac.at/index.php/jeacs/announcement/view/190), deadline 15 July 2025, now expired. Realistic historical fixtures prove the configured regular-call path; no current open regular CFP is fabricated. Actual thematic example [8.2 (2027), announcement 230](https://journals.univie.ac.at/index.php/jeacs/announcement/view/230) has abstract/paper dates and explicit guest editors. Later published papers alone do not close a still-open author call.

Reviewed aliases 190→174 and 195→175 are repeat announcements of the same call, confirmed from matching issue/theme/date evidence; they share a logical source key and original call identity. Other announcements retain stable numeric IDs. Known edition/year and the journal namespace qualify cycle identity; deadlines/publication/fetch times never identify a call. Unknown cycle remains unknown. Separate journal/theme/cycle fixtures must stay distinct. Directory/detail evidence for the same call combines without a second canonical. Known conflicting round dates retain both bounded evidence values for existing generic conflict normalization; missing evidence never overrides known values.

## Dates, metadata and scope

Date-only stays date-only, timezone unknown. Exact times require explicit source offsets/zones. Opening dates are submission-window metadata, not application deadlines. Abstract/submission/decision rounds are distinct. Explicit closed/withdrawn/upcoming/rolling states remain visible; expiry may close a genuine historical call conservatively, while published collections are excluded. A closed historical CFP is never presented as a fresh accepting opportunity. Upcoming maps to unknown coarse lifecycle plus explicit source state/window; no new lifecycle enum.

Capture journal/publisher/ISSN, original/journal URLs, bounded guest editor names without emails, explicit scope/topics, article restrictions, edition/window and source facts. Fetch policy each time to avoid a stale hard-coded fee claim. Fees are informational and carry their source/conditions; missing means unknown, never free by assumption. Publication calendar dates are observation evidence, not invented instants. Source-controlled links/contacts/scripts and full call prose are excluded from persistence/logging.
